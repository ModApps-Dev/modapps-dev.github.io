Odoo 19 Sales control

Correct confirmed orders without losing control.

Sales Order Amendments provides a controlled exception process before delivery or invoicing. Every request preserves the original values, requires approval by a different Sales Administrator and retains the audit history beside the order.

What it controls

A narrow, auditable correction workflow.

The module is designed for direct, unprocessed warehouse-to-customer deliveries. It stops when downstream work makes automatic correction unsafe.

Frozen comparison

Original and proposed dates and line values become immutable when the request is submitted.

Separation of duties

The requester cannot approve their own amendment. The rule is enforced on the server.

Atomic application

Odoo locks and revalidates the order before applying the approved proposal as one controlled operation.

Durable audit

Requester, approver, timestamps, reasons, decisions, before-and-after values and replaced deliveries remain available.

Standard Sales roles

Sales users request changes; Sales Administrators review company requests. No approval matrix setup is required.

Safe refusal

Invoices, payments, processed stock, special routes and stale source orders are rejected instead of guessed through.

Workflow

Five clear steps.

Request

Open a confirmed order, select Request Amendment, enter the reason and change the proposed date or line values.

Submit

A real difference is required. Submission freezes the original and proposed values for review.

Review

A different Sales Administrator opens the order's Amendments button and reviews every change.

Approve and apply

The server locks the order, checks its safety and freshness, then applies the change and rebuilds the direct delivery through standard Odoo logic.

Audit

The amendment and replaced delivery remain linked to the original order for later review.

Safety boundary

The module stops when automation is not safe.

It refuses orders with invoices or refunds, payments, down payments, picked or completed stock, returns, or source changes made after the snapshot.

It also refuses dropship, make-to-order, multi-step and special routes, configured or combo products, subscriptions, expense-derived lines, and services that create projects or tasks.
